## Full description (≤ 4000)

Some words are meant for later.

LaterLetter lets you write a letter today and choose the exact moment it opens — next week, on a graduation day, or ten years from now. Write to a friend, a partner, your family, or your future self.

A SEED FOR EVERY LETTER
Every letter plants a seed. While it waits, a little tree grows for you and another for the person you wrote to, and you can each watch the other's tree grow. Water it, bring it some sunlight, or take a quiet breathing moment together. It's all optional — there are no streaks and nothing withers if you forget.

SEALED UNTIL THE DAY
Once you seal a letter, it can't be edited, rescheduled or opened early — not by you, and not by us. Letters are encrypted before they are stored. On the day, the recipient breaks a wax seal and the letter unfolds.

THEY DON'T NEED THE APP YET
Write to anyone's email address. They get a gentle invitation, can claim their mailbox, and see a countdown to the day it opens. They can even open it on the web.

YOU CHOOSE WHAT THEY SEE
Before it opens, show your name and the date, add a subject or a short note — or stay a complete mystery.

CALM BY DESIGN
• Soft, warm design with a dark mode for quiet nights
• Original ambient music for writing, with background playback
• Gentle reminders only when something actually happens
• No ads, no feeds, no likes

SAFE AND PRIVATE
• Report any letter and block any sender at any time
• Personal addresses are never listed in a public directory
• Delete your account whenever you like, right in the app

LATERLETTER PLUS (optional subscription)
Your care counts double, unlock magical tree styles like Lantern and Starlight, and support a small, ad-free app. Plus only changes how your trees look — every letter still opens exactly on the date its sender chose.

For people aged 13 and over.
