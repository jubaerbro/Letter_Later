# Audio in LaterLetter

All music and sound effects in this folder are **original works generated procedurally** by
`scripts/generate-audio.mjs` (additive synthesis and filtered noise). No samples, loops or
recordings from third parties are used.

They are dedicated to the public domain under **CC0 1.0**. You may regenerate them at any
time with `npm run audio:generate` (requires ffmpeg).

User-chosen files ("Add your own music") stay on the user's device and are never uploaded.
The app does not stream commercial music.
